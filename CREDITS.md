## Rendering references

- **Burn, Lava, and Planet Hop:** David Hoskins, [*Hash without Sine*](https://www.shadertoy.com/view/4djSRW).
  See the third-party notices for the shader hash variants.
- **Bubble optics:** Chris Wyman, Peter-Pike Sloan, and Peter Shirley,
  [*Simple Analytic Approximations to the CIE XYZ Color Matching Functions*](https://jcgt.org/published/0002/02/01/),
  JCGT 2(2), 2013. The lookup implementation uses the fitting coefficients
  in Table 1.
- **Bubble highlights:** Michael Toksvig's [*Mipmapping Normal Maps*](https://doi.org/10.1080/2151237X.2005.10129203)
  is the reference for the specular antialiasing technique in `soap.glsl`.
